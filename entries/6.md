I can't believe the time between my last update and now is like the same length as between the start of Tera and this blog (Yeah the name is back to *Tera* now, no one liked the last name 😵‍💫). Anyway I figured now was a good time to update since I have no Wi-Fi in my house and can't work. And I think opening this thing again really puts everything in perspective, because 31 weeks is a long time to go without releasing... 🫠

So where am I now, 16 weeks later? After skimming through the last entries, Tera is definitely in a completely new stage, although some of the same sentiments still come through. Last time, I was just releasing it on the Testflight, and I actually got a lot of feedback both from friends and my own personal testing. I realized how unpolished I was from a full public release, and how many features just weren't fleshed out enough. Now, I'm glad to say that I finished implementing all major features around 4 weeks ago. While most of June was spent dealing with finals, graduation, celebrations, and moving, I was able to put in the hours in July and early August to fix many of the problems I saw with that beta release. 

But that begs the question, why haven't I put out a public release? And I think my decision to delay again hurts me the most since I really wanted to just get it out there and start actually growing the app; it really feels like I've waited way too long and my momentum is slowing down. However, I think it was the right call. In August, I had a couple trips which allowed me to take a step back, use the app as a user, and see things from a more neutral perspective, and I also had the opportunity to talk to some other start up founders and both get their feedback and hear about their experience. I think the main takeaways I've had from that regarding my app is that it is still lacking in UI/UX design and user onboarding. I think getting these two features right is actually more important than following the generic startup advice about just getting an MVP out there, since what these two issues really boil down to is making a **good first impression** and that is key for a consumer oriented service. So, I stand by my judgment and Tera will not be out on the official app store for another two weeks (and hopefully not much longer than that).

What kind of sucks about this though is UX design is something I probably am the least equipped to deal with, and as a result I've almost been pushing it away. For the past two weeks I've been preoccupied with a side project, and stuff keeps happening around me that's just kept me straying off course. It's probably the hardest issue I've had to tackle so far, which hasn't helped in keeping my motivation up. Discovering other travel apps in my niche has also slowed my momentum a little; although I've been told that having competitors is a good sign that I have a market, it's hard to feel good about my own creation when I'm seeing more polished showcases online of apps that are a little too close to home for my liking. The main thing about design though is that it might just be a skill I'll have to learn myself - I don't think there is any workaround. Having an intuitive design is something I can confidently say AI agents struggle with, and outsourcing the work isn't an option too. If there's one thing I've learned from dealing with people and this startup, it's that it is very hard to find someone willing to do committed, high-quality work that doesn't come with a price tag. I'm excited to be learning a new skill though; I think it'll come in useful no matter what happens with this app in the end. 

I think that's about it in terms of what's new with this app. One thing that's probably worth mentioning is I've moved to the Bay Area now, so I really am pursuing that tech founder dream and we'll just have to see what comes out of it. Life has almost been too comfortable lately, but I'll try to put in some serious grind so I can finally for real 100% get this app out on the App Store. I have some ideas for how I can start growing my userbase too so stay tuned. But yeah, hopefully I'll update this again not in 16 weeks and by then it'll be all ready.
