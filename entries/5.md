I'm excited to announce that my app will be submitted to Testflight (iOS beta app testing) in **the next 24 hours** if all goes well! Currently waiting on my designer to whip up a quick app logo that I can attach before sending it in. I took **Aventir** (yes new name still testing it out) on a test run on Saturday and honestly a lot of things went well. Here's the little share card my app made for me (still a bit rough around the edges but I thought it was kinda cool ☺️)

![Maiden voyage share](images/aventir-1st-share.png)

I think the craziest development in the last two weeks is that I've started to understand what it means to love your work and how people can actually work for hours at a job they like. Now I wouldn't say this is like my deepest passion or anything, but I do think that I've started to have a bit of an obsession with working (on my app) that makes it honestly kind of enjoyable. When I was just implementing features in my room for hours and chugging along, I was genuinely just vibing. I don't think I've ever been able to see things like how they are now really; I've always found work to be unengaging and play to be the actual fun. I honestly thought that all those people who talk about loving their job and never working a day were making stuff up but now I really do get it. I've found myself pushing off activities and staying up nights just to code more, and I think it's a really good sign that I might have found something that speaks to me more than past ventures into different fields. 

But I wouldn't say it's all sunshine and rainbows. I actually wrapped up Aventir version 1 for the most part around a week ago but I got cold feet making the switch to putting it out there. I think it's really not easy to put your own creation out into the real world despite its flaws, and knowing that people may judge your app on any possible detail and compare it to online monoliths like Instagram or Yelp and the like. I think that last minute decision to pull back actually turned out for the best since it gave me more clarity on my next few steps, but the uneasy feeling still remains. That last paragraph should come with an asterisk: While it was overwhelmingly easy and fun to work while I had a clear direction, it completely flipped once I had done most of the work and the rest is just dependent on the public. 😵‍💫

I think that's all I'll talk about this post because honestly there's just too much in my head in terms of this app. I see a lot of good outcomes and way more bad endings and all manners of potential issues, but I think the real truth of where Aventir is right now is probably somewhere in the middle and the only way I can get a clearer picture is just to put it out there and start getting some user input. I think I'll probably throw together a feedback form and a spreadsheet and keep the app invite-only, so I can really pinpoint where different opinions are coming from and their validity. 

I don't really have a progress bar now cause I finished with the goal, and I'm not sure if there's a clear next step. However, I think it might be good to have something to work towards still that I can visualize. I think the next goal will be **100 users** that actually use Aventir. 🤯 Although I do have 10 users, they can't actually access the app that much until tomorrow, so I'm giving it a 5%. Hopefully we'll start seeing some real growth in these coming weeks. 😁
